Ghanaian Local Food Guide Site Plan

A website plan for a beginner-friendly three-page project about traditional Ghanaian foods.

Site Name

Ghanaian Local Food Guide

This name was selected because the website will help visitors learn about popular Ghanaian local foods in a simple and organized way.

Optional domain: ghanafoodguide.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to introduce visitors to popular Ghanaian dishes. The site will provide food names, images, ingredients, cultural information, and simple recommendations for people who want to learn more about Ghanaian food.

Scenarios

  • What Ghanaian foods should I try if I am new to local Ghanaian dishes?
  • What ingredients are commonly used in foods like waakye, banku, fufu, and jollof rice?
  • How can I suggest my favorite Ghanaian food for the website?

Color Scheme

Deep Green #1f5f3b: Used for the header, footer, headings, and important accents.

Golden Yellow #d9a441: Used for borders, highlights, links, buttons, and small accent details.

The light cream background color will help the food content feel warm and readable.

Typography

Georgia: Used for main headings and section headings to give the site a clear and traditional feel.

Arial: Used for body text, lists, navigation, and form labels because it is simple and easy to read on screens.
